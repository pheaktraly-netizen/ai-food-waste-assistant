# Usability Evaluation Results

Evaluation was carried out by the designer (no external participants).

## Task baseline: add a food item
Starting from Home, timed from the first tap on Inventory to the tap on Save.

| Run | Time (seconds) | Taps | Completed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 | 3 | Yes |
| 2 | 6 | 3 | Yes |
| 3 | 9 | 3 | Yes |
| Mean | 10 | 3 | 3 of 3 |

Milestone 1 target (80% of users within 1 minute): not tested with users.

## Heuristic evaluation (Nielsen's ten heuristics)
| Screen | Heuristic | Problem found | Severity | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Login, Reset password | Match between system and real world | "Forget Password?" and "well send" typos | Low | [Not corrected] |
| Home | Consistency and standards | "Foodsaver" vs "FoodSaver"; "Recipe" vs "Recipes for you" | Low | [Not corrected] |
| Add Food | Match between system and real world | "Mannual" typo on the toggle | Low | [Not corrected] |
| Waste Statistics | Visibility of system status | "Log wasted item" has no destination | Medium | Not corrected (known limitation) |
| Item Detail | Error prevention | Delete has no confirmation step | Medium | Recommended for coded build |
| Add Food (barcode) | Visibility of system status | No scanning progress or failure state | Low | Recommended for coded build |
